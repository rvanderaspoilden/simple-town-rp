EcoEmoji TMP Sprite Asset — emoji artwork
=========================================

Emoji glyphs sourced from Twemoji.

  - 🌱 U+1F331 SEEDLING   (assets/72x72/1f331.png)
  - ⭐ U+2B50  WHITE MEDIUM STAR (assets/72x72/2b50.png)

Source: https://github.com/jdecked/twemoji  (maintained Twemoji fork)
Graphics license: CC-BY 4.0  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
Copyright 2020 Twitter, Inc and other contributors.

Usage in this project:
  - The two PNGs are composited into Assets/TextMesh Pro/Sprites/EcoEmoji.png (144x72 atlas).
  - Wrapped by the TMP Sprite Asset Assets/TextMesh Pro/Resources/Sprite Assets/EcoEmoji.asset
    (Unicode 0x1F331 -> "seedling", 0x2B50 -> "star").
  - Registered as an Emoji Fallback in TMP Settings (m_EmojiFallbackTextAssets), so raw emoji
    typed in any TMP text render as these sprites. Add more glyphs by extending the atlas + tables.
